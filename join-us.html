How to join

Come to a meeting anytime! If you enjoy your time, we welcome you to become a member. The current annual dues are $35 and payable at any meeting. We accept cash, personal check, Zelle, and Venmo.

Benefits

  • Access to some of the most talented woodworkers in the San Francisco Bay Area.
  • Summer Dinner meeting: Club buys dinner at a local restaurant.
  • Holiday Party: Club buys dinner at a local restuarant.
  • We do have a secret hand shake, but it’s so secret no one can remember it.
  • Lots of laughs!!
